# 9. Paper trading engine

The trading engine must simulate trades only.

- Maximum $2.50 initial position size.
- Maximum $0.50 daily loss.
- Maximum 1% simulated price impact.

- No borrowed funds.

The engine must simulate:

- Entry price
- Exit price
- Position quantity
- Network fees
- DEX or aggregator fees
- Slippage
- Price impact
- Token taxes where known
- Partial exits
- Remaining position quantity
- Realized P&L
- Unrealized P&L
- Net P&L after estimated costs

Clearly label estimated costs as estimates.

Do not assume that a paper trade can execute at the displayed market price. Use available read-only quotes and realistic execution assumptions.

Reject a paper order when:

- The token fails security rules.
- Market data is stale.
- Price impact exceeds the limit.
- The daily loss limit is reached.
- The maximum number of positions is already open.
- The required balance is unavailable.

# 10. Trading strategy

Implement the first strategy as:

**Strategy A — Conservative Momentum Pullback**

The process:

1. Discover a token within the previous six hours.
2. Observe it for at least 10 minutes.
3. Collect multiple market snapshots.
4. Verify liquidity and volume.
5. Run security checks.
6. Evaluate buying and selling activity.
7. Identify a meaningful upward trend.
8. Wait for a pullback rather than chasing a sudden spike.
9. Look for price stabilization and recovery.
10. Evaluate risk and estimated execution costs.
11. Enter a paper trade only when all configured conditions pass.

Avoid:

- Buying straight into a vertical candle.
- Entering a token with rapidly disappearing liquidity.
- Entering when data is stale.
- Entering on incomplete critical security checks.
- Overtrading.
- Forcing trades to fill available positions.

Make thresholds configurable through Settings.

## Initial exit rules

Implement configurable profit and risk management:

- At +30%: begin monitoring for profit protection.
- At +50%: sell 50% of the remaining simulated position.
- At +100%: close the remaining position.
- Exit earlier when there is a significant momentum breakdown, liquidity deterioration, or security warning.

Also implement configurable stop-loss and trailing-stop rules.

Make all exit operations idempotent. Never sell more quantity than the position actually holds.
